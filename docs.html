01 · overview

helus docs

helus is a launchpad where holders get paid. Every coin launched on helus starts with the same rules, written into its own pool on Meteora's bonding curve: a fee that starts at 99% and falls to 1% over two minutes, a creator who waits, and liquidity that's locked for good. The fees those rules collect are paid to the people holding the coin, every 10 minutes, in SOL.

Three ideas carry it:

  • Fast is expensive. Snipers can buy, but the first seconds cost almost everything they spend.
  • Holding earns. 40% of every fee goes to holders, weighted by how long they've held.
  • Rules don't move. They're set in the pool when a coin is created. Nobody can change them later, including us.

02 · the fee curve

The fee curve

The trading fee is set by the time a trade lands, counted from the coin's first block. It starts at 99% and falls on an exponential curve to 1% at two minutes, then stays at 1%.

fee(t) = 99% × (1/99)^(t / 120s)     for the first 120 seconds
fee(t) = 1%                          after that
Buy lands atFeeOf 1 SOL, buys tokens
block 099%0.01 SOL

1 minute9.9%0.90 SOL
2 minutes and later1%0.99 SOL

The curve is Meteora's own fee scheduler, part of the pool's settings. It applies to every trade in the pool, buys and sells, including the creator's.

03 · payouts

Payouts

Every 10 minutes the payout engine collects the coin's fees and pays the holders' share in SOL, straight to their wallets. There's nothing to claim and nothing to stake. A payout goes to every eligible wallet, split by weight:

weight = tokens held × multiplier (1x to 3x)
your payout = holders' share × your weight / everyone's weight   (at most 2% of the payout)

Wallets owed less than 0.001 SOL carry it forward to the next payout, so dust doesn't cost more in network fees than it's worth.

04 · holder rules

Holder rules

RuleWhat it does
Snipers don't collectWallets that bought in the first two minutes paid the fee and never earn payouts from that coin.
One funder, one shareWallets funded by the creator, or by the same wallet as another holder, are left out of payouts. Exchange withdrawals don't count as a shared funder.
Time multipliesEvery wallet starts at 1x and climbs to 3x over 24 hours of holding. A sell drops it back to 1x.
No whale takes it allOne wallet receives at most 2% of a payout. The rest is shared by everyone else.
The dev waits tooNo launch bag: the creator gets 3% of the supply, unlocked over 30 days after graduation, and liquidity is locked forever.

05 · orbits

Orbits

Your multiplier is your orbit. It climbs in a straight line from 1x at your first buy to 2x at 12 hours and 3x at 24 hours, and stays at 3x for as long as you hold. Buying more doesn't reset it. Selling any amount does, back to 1x.

i

The orbit is per wallet, per coin. Moving tokens to another wallet starts that wallet at 1x.

07 · launch settings

Launch settings

Every helus coin is created under one public config on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve. These are its settings:

Base feeExponential fee scheduler, 99% at launch to 1% at 120 seconds
Creator allocation3% of supply, locked and unlocked over 30 days from graduation
GraduationMigrates to a Meteora DAMM v2 pool; liquidity locked permanently
TokenFixed supply, no mint authority, no freeze authority
Creator fee shareSet so the split above holds after Meteora's cut

08 · the payout engine

The payout engine

The engine is a server that claims each coin's fees and sends the payouts. It's the one part of helus you trust us to keep running, so it's built to be checked:

  • It only ever holds fees, never anyone's coins.
  • Every payout is a public transaction with a memo naming the coin and the round.
  • Every round publishes its list of wallets, weights and amounts.

09 · replay

Replay

The replay reads any Solana coin's first two minutes from the chain and runs every buy through the helus fee curve: what each wallet spent, the fee it would have paid at that second, and whether it would be left out of payouts. It finds the block where trading opened, reads up to 180 transactions after it, and traces who funded the early wallets one step back.

It's a what-if. On helus, most of those buys wouldn't have happened at a 99% fee, which is the point.

11 · what's live

What's live

Live today: the replay, the calculator, launch drafts and the docs. Next: the helus config on Meteora, then launches and payouts. Nothing on this site shows a live coin until launches open.

12 · risks

Risks

  • Payouts follow volume. No trading, no fees, no payouts.
  • A fair start isn't a safe coin. Most coins go to zero, fair or not.
  • The engine is run by us. Every payout is public, but you're trusting it to keep running.
  • Paying holders can make a coin look like a security in some places.
!

Nothing here is financial advice. Read the rules, check the chain, and only use what you can afford to lose.
