How it works

ALT 040 · the problem

On most launchpads, the first two minutes pay the snipers.

A coin goes live and bots buy in the same block. They sell into the first real buyers, and the people who stay are left holding a chart that was decided before they arrived. Holding earns nothing. Flipping earns everything.

ALT 090 · the orbit

01 / 06

A coin launches on helus.

Its rules are written into its own pool on Meteora the moment it's created. Nobody can change them afterwards: not the creator, not us.

02 / 06

Snipers burn up on entry.

The fee starts at 99% and falls to 1% over two minutes. A bot that buys in the first second keeps 1% of what it spends. The rest stays behind.

03 / 06

What they paid becomes the bounty.

Every early fee and every trading fee after it lands in the coin's bounty. Half of it, after Meteora's cut, belongs to the holders.

04 / 06

Every 10 minutes, the holders get paid.

The protocol pays the bounty out in SOL to every wallet in orbit. Nothing to claim, nothing to stake. It just lands.

05 / 06

Hold longer, orbit higher.

Your share grows from 1x to 3x over your first day of holding. The wallets that stay the longest earn the most from every payout.

06 / 06

Sell, and you fall out of orbit.

Selling resets your orbit to 1x. You can come back any time, but you start again from the ground.

The money

ALT 220 · one payout

Every 10 minutes, the same five steps.

1

Collect

The engine claims the fees the pool took since the last round.

2

Snapshot

Every wallet holding the coin, minus the pool, the snipers and the shared funders.

3

Weigh

Each wallet's tokens times its orbit, from 1x to 3x.

4

Cap

No wallet gets more than 2%. The rest carries into the next round.

5

Send

SOL goes out to every wallet, with a public record of the round.

ALT 270 · the rules

Five rules. Every coin. No settings.

1

Snipers don't collect.

Wallets that buy in the first two minutes paid the fee and earn nothing from payouts. They can hold, they just don't get paid for it.

2

One funder, one share.

Wallets funded by the dev, or by the same funder as another holder, are left out. Twenty wallets from one hand earn like zero.

3

Time multiplies.

Every wallet starts at 1x and climbs to 3x over 24 hours of holding. A sell drops it back to 1x.

4

No whale takes it all.

One wallet can receive at most 2% of a payout. Whatever is above the cap is shared by everyone else.

5

The dev waits too.

No launch bag. The creator gets 3% of the supply, unlocked over 30 days after graduation, and liquidity is locked forever.

ALT 412 · questions

The questions worth asking first.

Is helus live?

Partly, and we'd rather say exactly which part. The replay and the calculator work today. Launches and payouts open when the helus config is live on Meteora. Nothing on this page is a live coin.

Who enforces the fee curve and the locks?

Meteora's bonding curve program does. The 99% to 1% fee, the creator's unlock and the liquidity lock are settings of the coin's pool, set when it's created. Nobody can change them afterwards.

Who sends the payouts?

The helus payout engine. It collects the pool's fees and pays holders in SOL every 10 minutes. It only ever holds fees, never anyone's coins, and every payout is a public transaction you can check.

Can a sniper still buy?

Yes. Nothing stops a buy, it just costs up to 99% in the first seconds. On helus the fastest wallet doesn't win. It pays.

What if I sell and buy back?

You can. Selling drops you to 1x, and you climb back up from your next buy. The multiplier rewards holding, not timing.

Is this financial advice?

No. Payouts depend on trading volume, and most coins go to zero, fair or not. Paying holders can also make a coin look like a security in some places. Read the docs before you launch or buy.
